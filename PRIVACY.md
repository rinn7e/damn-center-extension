# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 1, 2026_

This policy covers the Damn Center browser extension for Chrome, Firefox and
Safari.

## The short version

Damn Center doesn't collect, transmit, sell or share any data. It has no
servers, makes no network requests and contains no analytics or tracking.

## What the extension stores

To remember your preferences, Damn Center saves these settings **on your
device**, in your browser's local extension storage:

- the URL patterns ("matches") you create, and the website names (hostnames)
  they belong to
- padding width, side, colors, background patterns and theme mode
- general preferences such as the ruler, the popup font size and whether the
  extension is turned on

These settings never leave your device. They aren't synced to your browser
account, and the developer can't see them.

## What the extension reads

To apply your settings, the extension's content script checks the address
(URL) of each page you visit and compares it with your saved matches, entirely
inside your browser. It doesn't read page content, form fields, passwords or
cookies, and it doesn't record your browsing history.

## Exporting and importing

**Export** saves your settings to a JSON file on your computer, and **Import**
reads a file you choose. Both happen locally; nothing is uploaded.

## Removing your data

Uninstalling the extension deletes all of its stored settings. You can also
remove individual matches at any time from the popup.
