Center any website on your widescreen or ultrawide monitor, with per-site padding, rulers, themes and background patterns.

Damn Center (the page!) moves off-center website layouts to the middle of your screen, so reading on a widescreen or ultrawide monitor no longer strains your neck and eyes. It adds customizable left and right padding to web pages on the fly. Adjust padding width, select side shifting, and align layout grids. Customize base colors, select beautiful SVG background patterns (grids, dots, stripes, carbon fiber, lattice), and automatically sync with system Light/Dark mode.

Includes a vertical split alignment ruler and a smart window maximization detector that suspends padding automatically when the browser window is tiled or resized to optimize screen space.
